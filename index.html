process

From idea to object

Every piece moves through the same four steps.

Sketch

An everyday object, a memory or a problem worth solving becomes a drawing.

Model

The form is built and refined in 3D until the proportions feel right.

Melt

Filament is melted and laid down, layer by layer, into the final shape.

Finish

Each piece is checked, cleaned up and packed by hand.
